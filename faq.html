Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about HexaTP's transfer pricing solutions, services, and capabilities.

About HexaTP

What is HexaTP? +

HexaTP is a transfer pricing intelligence platform that acts as a co-sourced TP delivery partner, helping firms and tax teams deliver defensible transfer pricing work across jurisdictions.

Who does HexaTP work with? +

HexaTP supports CA firms, law firms, advisory partners, and multinational tax teams with transfer pricing documentation, benchmarking, TP policy support, and audit-ready reporting.

How experienced is the HexaTP team? +

HexaTP brings over 100 combined years of transfer pricing experience and has supported 500+ TP clients worldwide.

What kind of team does HexaTP have? +

HexaTP's multi-disciplinary team includes lawyers, economists, CAs, cost accountants, CPAs, MBAs, and senior TP professionals, combining legal, accounting, economic, and business insight.

Services & Coverage

How many jurisdictions does HexaTP cover? +

HexaTP provides transfer pricing solutions across 50+ jurisdictions, with a global intelligence platform supporting cross-border compliance and tax optimization.

What services does HexaTP offer? +

HexaTP offers transfer pricing documentation, risk mitigation and health checks, benchmarking through its proprietary database, advisory and litigation support, and TP due diligence services.

Is HexaTP's work aligned with international standards? +

Yes, HexaTP's documentation and advisory work is OECD and BEPS aligned, ensuring compliance with global transfer pricing standards.

Hexanomy & Technology

What is Hexanomy? +

Hexanomy is HexaTP's proprietary database, encompassing financial data on over 100,000 companies, supplemented by access to public financial databases and industry directories for comprehensive TP analysis.

Specialized Services

What does HexaTP's risk mitigation service include? +

It includes deep dive health checks to identify intercompany transaction risks before tax authorities do, covering gap analysis, audit defense, and policy tuning.

Does HexaTP provide TP litigation support? +

Yes. HexaTP helps design and implement global TP policy and review agreements for multinational corporations, and also provides transfer pricing litigation support services.
